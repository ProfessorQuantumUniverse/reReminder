reReminder 4.0.1 - hotfix

- Time windows respect the interval (#15): a window no longer restarts the reminder at its opening. The interval alone decides when the next event is; the window only decides whether it may go off.
- No more early alerts ahead of a window opening or at the end of a window.
- Tip: to line events up with a window, set the start to its opening.
- Alarms that cannot cover the screen fall back to a notification with sound.
