This is a major update with a complete rewrite of the app!

- **Complete Redesign:** The entire user interface has been rebuilt from the ground up using modern Jetpack Compose for a fluid and intuitive experience.
- **Enhanced Settings:** A brand-new settings screen gives you more control over your notifications, including custom text, sound vs. Text-to-Speech, and different vibration patterns.
- **Improved Stability:** Alarms and notifications are now more reliable.
- **Permission Handling:** The app now properly requests necessary permissions on modern Android versions.
- **"Don't Kill My App" Integration:** Includes a helpful one-time warning for users on devices with aggressive background process management to ensure reminders are delivered reliably.
