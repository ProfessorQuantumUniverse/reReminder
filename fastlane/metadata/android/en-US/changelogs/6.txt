reReminder 3.1.1 - polish and fixes.

- Countdowns no longer flash the whole line every second; only the changing digits roll.
- Interval presets now apply exactly as tapped.
- Weekday pills no longer bump into each other.
- Title no longer flickers at the end of a list.
- Smoother closing of screens, incl. the back gesture.
- Delete dialog closes right away.
- Double taps can no longer save a reminder twice or close the app to an empty screen.
- Countdowns no longer skip seconds.
