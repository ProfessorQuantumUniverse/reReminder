<strong>reReminder</strong> helps you stay focused and mindful with simple, recurring notifications. Whether you need a nudge to correct your posture, take a break, or practice mindfulness, reReminder has you covered.<br><br>

<strong>Features:</strong><br>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Customizable Intervals:</strong> Set reminders for any interval, from minutes to hours.</li>
  <li><strong>Flexible Notifications:</strong> Choose between a standard notification sound or have the reminder text read aloud with Text-to-Speech.</li>
  <li><strong>Personalized Alerts:</strong> Customize the notification title, text, and vibration pattern to fit your needs.</li>
  <li><strong>Simple Interface:</strong> A clean, intuitive interface built with Jetpack Compose makes setting up reminders effortless.</li>
  <li><strong>Dark Mode:</strong> The app respects your system's theme for comfortable use in any lighting.</li>
</ul>
